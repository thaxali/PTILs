## Why It Works

Generic workout plans don't account for your specific situation. The AI adapts exercise selection, volume, and intensity based on your actual constraints.

## Example Output

**Your profile:** Beginner, 3 days/week available, home gym with dumbbells.

**Week 1-4 Plan:**
Mon: Upper body (3x10 push-ups, 3x12 dumbbell rows...)
Wed: Lower body (3x12 goblet squats...)
Fri: Full body circuit...

**Progression:** Add 1 rep per set each week.
