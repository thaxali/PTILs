## Why It Works

Raw notes capture everything but organize nothing. Having the AI identify themes, group related ideas, and extract decisions turns chaos into clarity.

## Example Output

## Product Direction
- Consensus: Focus on B2B before B2C
- Key insight: Enterprise customers want self-hosted option

## Technical Decisions

- Deploy on AWS (team has existing experience)

## Open Questions
- Pricing model still undecided (subscription vs. usage-based)
- Need legal review of data processing agreement
