## Why It Works

Most people don't budget because it feels overwhelming. Having someone structure the numbers, categorize spending, and identify obvious savings makes it actionable.

## Example Output

**Income:** $6,000/mo after tax

**Recommended split (50/30/20):**
- Needs: $3,000 (rent $1,800, groceries $400, insurance $200...)
- Wants: $1,800 (dining, entertainment, subscriptions)
- Save/Invest: $1,200

**Quick wins:** Your 5 streaming subscriptions cost $75/mo. You watch 2 of them. Save $45/mo = $540/year...
