## Why It Works

You can't see your own product with fresh eyes. Simulating a first-time user reveals confusing UX, missing context, and broken assumptions that insiders are blind to.

## Example Output

**Step 1: Landing page**
'I see a tagline but I still don't know what this does. Is it a tool? A service? The hero image doesn't help.'

**Step 2: Signup**
'Why do you need my company size during signup? I just want to try it. This feels like a sales funnel, not a product.'

**Step 3: Dashboard**
'Too many options. Where do I start? I'd expect a 'Get Started' wizard or a sample project...'
