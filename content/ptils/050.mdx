## Why It Works

Opinion writing requires a clear point of view, evidence, and persuasive structure. This prompt combines all three to produce publishable-quality commentary.

## Example Output

**Topic: Why every developer should write**

The best engineers I know are also the best writers. Not because writing is their hobby — but because clear writing forces clear thinking. When you can't explain your architecture in prose, you don't understand it yet...
