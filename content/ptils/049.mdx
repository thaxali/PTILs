## Why It Works

Having immediate access to evidence-based coping strategies when you need them most can be genuinely helpful. The AI draws from established therapeutic frameworks.

## Example Output

**Technique: 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding**
When anxiety spikes, name: 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. This interrupts the spiral by forcing your brain into the present moment.

**Reframe exercise:** Write down the anxious thought, then ask: 'What would I tell a friend who said this?'
