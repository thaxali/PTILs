## Why It Works

Improv-style brainstorming suspends judgment and expands possibilities. Criticism kills ideas early; 'yes, and' lets them grow first.

## Example Output

**Yes, and** what if you also made it multiplayer?
**Yes, and** what if there was a leaderboard?
**Yes, and** what if the top contributors got featured...
