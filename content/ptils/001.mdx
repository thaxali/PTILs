## Why It Works

Self-critique forces the model to evaluate from a reader's perspective before revising. This mimics the revision process of expert writers and often produces significantly better output than a single pass.

## Example Output

**First draft:** Our app helps you manage tasks...

**Critique:** This is generic and doesn't highlight unique value...

**Improved:** Take back your mornings. TaskFlow uses AI to prioritize what actually matters...
