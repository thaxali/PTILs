## Why It Works

OKRs fail when objectives are vague or key results aren't measurable. The AI enforces the structure — ambitious objectives + specific numbers — that makes OKRs effective.

## Example Output

**Objective 1:** Become the go-to tool for developer onboarding
- KR1: Increase new user activation rate from 34% to 55%
- KR2: Reduce time-to-first-value from 45 min to under 15 min
- KR3: Achieve NPS of 50+ among users in their first week

**Objective 2:** Build a self-sustaining community...
