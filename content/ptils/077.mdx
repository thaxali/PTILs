## Why It Works

Proofreading is different from editing. This prompt constrains the AI to only fix objective errors, preserving your voice while catching the mistakes your eyes skip over.

## Example Output

**Original:** 'Their going to the store tommorrow, they should of called ahead.'

**Corrected:** 'They're going to the store tomorrow, they should have called ahead.'

**Fixes:** their→they're (contraction), tommorrow→tomorrow (spelling), should of→should have (grammar)
