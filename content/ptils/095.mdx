## Why It Works

Generic cover letters get ignored. Connecting your specific experience to the company's specific situation shows you've done your homework and can articulate fit.

## Example Output

I noticed [Company] just launched its API marketplace — I've spent the last 3 years building exactly this kind of developer platform at [Previous Company], growing it from 50 to 2,000 active integrations.

What excites me about this role isn't just the tech. It's that you're betting on developers as your growth channel...
