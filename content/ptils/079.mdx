## Why It Works

Interior design requires balancing aesthetics, function, budget, and personal taste. The AI considers all constraints simultaneously and suggests cohesive design systems rather than random items.

## Example Output

**Room:** Living room, 15x12 ft, $2K budget, minimalist style

**Color scheme:** White walls + warm wood + one sage green accent wall

**Key pieces:**
- Low-profile sofa in light gray ($600) — face toward window
- Round walnut coffee table ($200) — breaks up angular furniture
- Floor lamp with warm bulb ($80) — no overhead lighting, creates coziness...
