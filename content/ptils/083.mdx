## Why It Works

Headlines determine whether anyone reads your content. Having 10 options with different formats lets you A/B test or pick the one that best matches your audience.

## Example Output

**Topic:** Why remote work is here to stay

1. Remote Work Won. Here's the Data.
2. 7 Stats That Prove the Office Is Never Coming Back
3. Why Your Best Employees Will Quit If You Mandate Return-to-Office
4. The Remote Work Debate Is Over (And the Numbers Are Clear)
5. Still Forcing People Back to the Office? Read This First.
