## Why It Works

A life coach helps you see the forest when you're stuck in the trees. The AI can suggest frameworks for decision-making and habit-building that you might not discover on your own.

## Example Output

**Your situation:** You want to switch careers but feel stuck.

**Strategy 1:** Don't quit yet. Start a '5% experiment' — spend 5% of your week (2 hours) on the new direction. Low risk, real data.

**Strategy 2:** Talk to 3 people already doing what you want. Most career anxiety comes from imagining, not knowing...
