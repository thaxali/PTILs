## Why It Works

Competitive analysis reveals market gaps and positioning opportunities. A structured comparison across multiple dimensions helps you find where competitors are weak and where you can differentiate.

## Example Output

| Feature | You | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|---------|-----|-------------|-------------|
| Real-time collab | Yes | No | Yes |
| Free tier | 3 projects | 1 project | None |

**Gap opportunity:** None of them offer async collaboration with video — this is a whitespace you could own...
