## Why It Works

User personas in feedback prompts surface objections you'd miss. Different personas catch different issues.

## Example Output

**As a skeptical enterprise buyer:**

'Sounds great, but I've heard this before. My concerns:

1. Where's the case study from a company my size?
2. 'AI-powered' means what exactly?...'
