## Why It Works

Etymology reveals hidden connections between words and ideas. Understanding origins makes vocabulary stick and gives you deeper insight into language.

## Example Output

**Word: 'Salary'**

From Latin 'salarium' — literally 'salt money.' Roman soldiers were sometimes paid in salt, which was valuable for preserving food. This is also where 'worth your salt' comes from. The meaning shifted from literal salt payment to any regular compensation by the medieval period.
