## Why It Works

Multiple analogies from different domains reveal different facets of the concept. One of them will click for any given learner.

## Example Output

**Cooking analogy:** APIs are like a restaurant menu. You don't need to know how the kitchen works — you just order from the menu and get what you asked for...

**Sports analogy:** APIs are like the rules of basketball...
