## Why It Works

Restricting vocabulary forces truly simple explanations. If you can't explain it in common words, you don't fully understand it — this prompt tests that.

## Example Output

**Concept: Encryption**

Imagine you write a letter but change every letter to a different one using a secret plan. Only the person who knows your plan can read the letter. If someone else finds it, they just see a mess of letters that means nothing. That's what computers do with your messages and money information to keep it safe.
