## Why It Works

Generic feedback like 'make it clearer' doesn't help. Pointing to specific sentences and offering concrete rewrites teaches you to recognize and fix your own patterns.

## Example Output

**Your sentence:** 'The utilization of this methodology enables optimization of outcomes.'

**Feedback:** This is corporate fog. Every word is abstract. Try: 'This method gets better results.' Same meaning, half the words, twice the clarity.
