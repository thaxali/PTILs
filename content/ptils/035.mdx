## Why It Works

Seeing both sides of an argument structured and articulated helps you form better opinions, prepare for discussions, and identify the strongest points on each side.

## Example Output

**Topic:** Remote work vs. office work

**For remote:** Studies show 13% productivity increase, elimination of commute...

**For office:** Serendipitous collaboration, stronger culture, clearer work-life boundaries...

**Conclusion:** The evidence suggests hybrid models capture most benefits of both...
