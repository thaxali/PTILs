## Why It Works

Consistent brand voice builds trust and recognition. A concrete guide with examples — not just adjectives — ensures everyone on the team writes in the same voice.

## Example Output

**Brand personality:** Confident, warm, slightly nerdy

**We say:** 'Here's what we found' (direct)
**We don't say:** 'We are pleased to inform you' (corporate)

**Do:** Use contractions, be specific, use analogies
**Don't:** Use jargon, hedge with 'perhaps', use exclamation marks

**Words to avoid:** leverage, synergy, disrupt, game-changing
