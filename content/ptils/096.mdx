## Why It Works

Effective studying isn't about hours — it's about structure. Spaced repetition, interleaving, and scheduled review are evidence-based techniques that the AI can plan optimally.

## Example Output

**Exam:** AWS Solutions Architect in 4 weeks

**Week 1 Focus:** Foundations (EC2, S3, VPC)
Mon: EC2 (2hr) — new material
Tue: S3 (2hr) — new material
Wed: EC2 review (30min) + VPC (2hr)
Thu: S3 review (30min) + Practice questions
Fri: All three — practice exam

**Priority:** VPC and IAM need extra time (most exam questions)...
