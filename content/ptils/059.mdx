## Why It Works

Academic structure forces rigorous thinking. Even for non-academic topics, the abstract-methods-findings format produces clear, well-organized analysis.

## Example Output

**Abstract:** This paper examines the effectiveness of spaced repetition in language learning across 15 studies published between 2018-2024...

**Key Finding:** Spaced repetition improved long-term retention by 47% compared to massed practice (p < 0.001)...
