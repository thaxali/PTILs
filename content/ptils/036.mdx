## Why It Works

Screenwriting requires specific formatting and pacing that AI handles well. You get properly structured scenes with dialogue, action lines, and dramatic beats.

## Example Output

SARAH (30s, sharp eyes, fidgeting with her phone) sits across from MARK (40s, calm, overdressed for a coffee meeting).

SARAH: You said you had something important.

MARK: (sliding a folder across the table) I found your sister...
