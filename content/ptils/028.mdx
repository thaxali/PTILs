## Why It Works

Practicing interviews with AI lets you rehearse answers, get feedback, and build confidence without the pressure of a real interview. The one-at-a-time format mimics actual interview flow.

## Example Output

**Interviewer:** Tell me about yourself.

*[You answer]*

**Interviewer:** That's interesting. Can you tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult technical challenge?
