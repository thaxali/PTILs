## Why It Works

Persuasive writing follows a proven structure: hook, claim, evidence, counterargument, resolution. This prompt ensures all elements are present and properly ordered.

## Example Output

**Hook:** Every 40 seconds, someone in America has a stroke. Most of them didn't know they were at risk.

**Claim:** Universal preventive health screenings would save 100,000 lives annually...

**Counterargument:** Critics cite cost, but the math is clear: prevention costs $1 for every $7 spent on treatment...
