## Why It Works

History is about connections, not just dates. Analyzing events through multiple lenses (cultural, economic, political) reveals patterns and lessons applicable to today.

## Example Output

**Event: The 1918 Spanish Flu**

**Economic impact:** GDP dropped 6-8% in affected countries. But the recovery was remarkably fast — a 'V-shaped' rebound by 1920.

**Social impact:** Paradoxically, it accelerated public health infrastructure. Cities that locked down earliest recovered fastest economically...
