## Why It Works

Meal planning that accounts for calories, macros, dietary restrictions, and glycemic index is complex. The AI handles the math while you focus on what sounds appetizing.

## Example Output

**Lunch: Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl (480 cal)**
- 1 cup cooked quinoa (220 cal, GI: 53)
- ½ cup chickpeas (130 cal)
- Cucumber, tomato, feta, olive oil dressing

**Macros:** 18g protein, 52g carbs, 22g fat
