## Why It Works

Style mimicry helps when you need a specific tone. It's also a great way to make dry topics more engaging by channeling a compelling voice.

## Example Output

**In the style of David Foster Wallace:**

So here's the thing about machine learning, and it's the kind of thing that seems obvious once you think about it but which nobody actually thinks about...
