## Why It Works

Having a patient, always-available conversation partner who corrects mistakes in real-time accelerates language learning. The 100-word limit keeps responses digestible.

## Example Output

**You:** 'I go to store yesterday and buyed some foods.'

**AI:** 'I went to the store yesterday and bought some food.' — Note: 'go' becomes 'went' in past tense, and 'food' is typically uncountable. What kind of food did you buy?
