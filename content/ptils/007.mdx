## Why It Works

Constraints force precision. Without them, summaries tend to be verbose. Specific word/sentence limits make output immediately usable.

## Example Output

• The study found remote workers were 13% more productive than office counterparts.
• Collaboration suffered, with team projects taking 20% longer to complete.
• Hybrid models (2-3 days remote) showed the best balance of both metrics.
