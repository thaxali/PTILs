## Why It Works

Most release notes are written for developers, not users. Leading with benefits ('You can now X') instead of features ('Added support for Y') makes users care.

## Example Output

## What's New in v2.4

**Big improvements:**
- **Find anything instantly** — Search now works across all your projects, not just the current one
- **No more lost work** — Auto-save kicks in every 30 seconds (was 5 minutes)

**Small but nice:**
- Dark mode now applies to the editor toolbar (finally)
- Export to PDF includes images (previously text-only)
