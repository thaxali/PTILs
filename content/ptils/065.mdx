## Why It Works

Choosing the wrong chart type obscures your data story. The AI matches your data shape to the right visualization and explains the reasoning, teaching you visualization principles.

## Example Output

**Your data:** Monthly revenue for 12 months

**Best format:** Line chart (shows trend over time)

```
Revenue ($K)
50 |          ╱‾‾
40 |     ╱‾‾╱
30 |  ╱‾╱
20 |╱╱
   +----------→
   J F M A M J
```
**Insight:** Clear upward trend with acceleration in Q2.
