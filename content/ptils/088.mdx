## Why It Works

Sales skills improve through practice against realistic resistance. The AI creates a safe space to rehearse pitches, handle objections, and refine your value proposition.

## Example Output

**Customer:** So, what are you selling?

*[You pitch]*

**Customer:** Interesting, but we already use [competitor]. What makes yours different?

*[You respond]*

**Customer:** That sounds great in theory, but I've been burned by tools that promise AI magic. Can you show me a concrete example?
