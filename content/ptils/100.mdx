## Why It Works

Bad interview questions confirm what you already believe. Good questions uncover what you don't know. This prompt generates non-leading questions that reveal genuine user behavior.

## Example Output

1. Walk me through the last time you had to [task]. What was that like?
2. What tools do you currently use for this? What's frustrating about them?
3. If this problem magically disappeared tomorrow, what would change about your work?
4. Have you tried to solve this before? What happened?
5. How much time do you spend on this per week?...
