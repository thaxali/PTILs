## Why It Works

Chain-of-thought prompting improves accuracy on complex tasks by forcing the model to show intermediate steps rather than jumping to conclusions.

## Example Output

Let me work through this step by step:

**Step 1:** First, I need to understand...
**Step 2:** Given that, the next consideration is...
