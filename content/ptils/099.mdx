## Why It Works

Difficult emails get procrastinated because we can't find the right tone. Having someone else draft it first breaks the paralysis and gives you something to edit rather than create from scratch.

## Example Output

**Situation:** Telling a client the project will be 2 weeks late.

Subject: Project timeline update — revised delivery date

Hi [Name],

I want to be upfront: we're going to need two additional weeks to deliver [project]. Here's why, and here's what we're doing about it.

[Reason — honest, not excuse-making]

The new delivery date is [date]. To make up for the delay, we're [concrete offering]...
