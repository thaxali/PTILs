## Why It Works

Retrospectives are only valuable if they produce specific takeaways. The structured format ensures you capture both successes to repeat and failures to prevent.

## Example Output

**What went well:**
- Shipped 2 days early by cutting scope to MVP
- Daily standups kept everyone aligned

**What didn't:**
- Testing was squeezed into the last 3 days — found 2 critical bugs
- Stakeholder feedback came too late to incorporate

**Action items:**
1. Allocate 20% of timeline to testing from day 1
2. Schedule stakeholder review at 50% completion, not 90%
