## Why It Works

A coach who knows your context can offer tailored strategies instead of generic advice. The AI adapts its suggestions to your specific situation and challenges.

## Example Output

**You:** I want to start a side project but I keep procrastinating.

**Coach:** Let's start small. Commit to just 15 minutes tonight — set a timer. The hardest part is starting. Also, tell one friend about your project today. External accountability works...
