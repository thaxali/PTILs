## Why It Works

Few-shot examples are more precise than adjectives. Instead of saying 'witty and sarcastic,' showing examples calibrates the exact tone.

## Example Output

1. 'Your AI assistant doesn't have hallucinations, it has creative differences.'
2. 'Just prompted ChatGPT to write my OKRs. Finally, corporate speak that makes sense.'...
