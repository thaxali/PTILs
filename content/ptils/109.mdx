## Why It Works

Bad onboarding loses employees. A structured 30-day plan with clear milestones helps new hires feel supported and productive rather than lost and overwhelmed.

## Example Output

**Day 1:**
- [ ] Laptop and accounts set up before arrival
- [ ] Welcome lunch with direct team
- [ ] 1:1 with manager: 30-day expectations

**Week 1:**
- [ ] Shadow 3 customer calls
- [ ] Read product docs + complete internal course
- [ ] Coffee chats with 5 cross-functional teammates

**Week 2-3:**
- [ ] First small project (low-stakes, real impact)
- [ ] Present findings to team...
