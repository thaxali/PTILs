## Why It Works

Investment advice is often generic. Providing your specific numbers and risk profile lets the AI suggest a concrete allocation rather than vague principles.

## Example Output

**Profile:** Age 30, medium risk, $50K saved, $500/mo to invest

**Suggested allocation:**
- 60% US Total Stock Market Index (e.g., VTI)
- 20% International Index (e.g., VXUS)
- 10% Bond Index (e.g., BND)
- 10% REIT Index (e.g., VNQ)

**Why:** At 30, you have 35 years of compounding. Heavy equity weighting is appropriate because you can ride out volatility...
