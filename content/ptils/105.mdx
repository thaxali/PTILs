## Why It Works

Most meetings generate ideas but not accountability. Extracting action items with owners and dates converts vague discussion into concrete next steps.

## Example Output

**Action items:**
1. [ ] Sarah: Send revised pricing proposal to client → by Friday
2. [ ] Dev team: Fix checkout bug (critical) → by EOD Wednesday
3. [ ] Alex: Schedule user research sessions → by next Monday

**Unresolved decisions:**
- Do we launch V2 before or after the conference? (Need VP input)
- Pricing tier structure — revisit Thursday
