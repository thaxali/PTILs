## Why It Works

Salary negotiation is uncomfortable for most people. Having a coach draft the actual email with data-backed justification removes the emotional barrier and produces better outcomes.

## Example Output

**Subject:** Excited About the Offer — A Few Thoughts

'Thank you for the offer — I'm genuinely excited about the role. Based on my research of market rates for this position in [city] (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi), and given my [X years] of experience with [specific skill], I'd like to propose a base salary of...'
