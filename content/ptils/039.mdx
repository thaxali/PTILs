## Why It Works

Poetry benefits from constraint and precision. Specifying the theme while asking for emotional depth and craft pushes the AI beyond generic verse into something resonant.

## Example Output

**The Weight of Empty Chairs**

The table set for four,
three plates collecting dust like years.
I still pour the extra glass of wine —
force of habit, or of hope.
