## Why It Works

A structured review framework ensures you cover all aspects of a film. The 'how it made you feel' instruction adds the subjective perspective that makes reviews compelling.

## Example Output

**The Holdovers** feels like a warm sweater on a cold day. Alexander Payne crafts a holiday film that earns its emotions through character rather than sentiment. Paul Giamatti's crusty professor is both infuriating and heartbreaking...
