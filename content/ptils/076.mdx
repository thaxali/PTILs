## Why It Works

Music discovery algorithms are great but opaque. Asking AI to explain why songs are similar gives you insight into musical patterns and helps you articulate your taste.

## Example Output

**Song:** 'Midnight City' by M83

**Playlist:** 'Neon Nostalgia'
**Description:** Synth-driven tracks that feel like driving through a city at night.

1. 'Somebody Else' — The 1975
2. 'Digital Love' — Daft Punk
3. 'Electric Feel' — MGMT
4. 'Heart It Races' — Architecture in Helsinki...
