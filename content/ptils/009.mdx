## Why It Works

Pre-mortems surface risks that optimism bias hides. By assuming failure, you identify vulnerabilities you'd otherwise overlook.

## Example Output

**Post-mortem from the future:**

1. We underestimated the technical complexity of...
2. The market shifted when competitor X...
3. Team burnout became critical after...
