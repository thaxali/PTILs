## Why It Works

Effective teaching requires structure, not just knowledge. A lesson plan with objectives, activities, and assessment ensures your audience actually learns, not just listens.

## Example Output

**Topic:** Intro to Design Thinking | **Audience:** College students

**Objectives:** Define the 5 stages, apply empathy mapping, prototype one solution.

**0-10 min:** Hook — show a badly designed product. Ask: 'What would you fix?'
**10-25 min:** Explain the 5 stages with real examples
**25-45 min:** Activity — students empathy-map a problem in pairs
**45-55 min:** Share solutions, vote on most creative
**55-60 min:** Reflection prompt...
