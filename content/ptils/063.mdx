## Why It Works

Consistent social media requires a system, not just random posts. A content strategy with pillars and schedules turns social media from a chore into a repeatable process.

## Example Output

**Content Pillars:**
1. Educational (40%) — Tips, how-tos, industry insights
2. Behind-the-scenes (25%) — Team stories, process reveals
3. Community (20%) — User spotlights, polls, Q&As
4. Promotional (15%) — Product updates, launches

**Schedule:** Mon/Wed/Fri on all platforms. LinkedIn gets longer-form on Tuesdays...
