## Why It Works

We all have blind spots in our reasoning. A systematic fallacy check catches errors in logic that sound convincing but don't hold up — like correlation-causation confusion or appeal to authority.

## Example Output

**Statement:** 'This diet worked for my friend, so it will work for everyone.'

**Fallacy:** Hasty generalization + anecdotal evidence. One person's experience doesn't establish a pattern. Clinical studies with thousands of participants still show mixed results for most diets...
