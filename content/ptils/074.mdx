## Why It Works

Accessibility issues are easy to miss if you're not a screen reader user. A systematic WCAG audit catches issues that affect real users and can also have legal implications.

## Example Output

**Issues found:**

1. **Critical:** Images missing alt text (WCAG 1.1.1)
2. **Serious:** Color contrast ratio 2.8:1 on muted text — needs 4.5:1 minimum (WCAG 1.4.3)
3. **Moderate:** No skip-to-content link for keyboard users
4. **Minor:** Links say 'click here' instead of describing the destination
