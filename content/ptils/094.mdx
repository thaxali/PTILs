## Why It Works

Great speeches follow a structure: story → lesson → call to action. The 'not preachy' instruction keeps the tone authentic rather than overwrought.

## Example Output

Three years ago, I almost quit. Not dramatically — no letter of resignation. I just... stopped trying. I showed up, did the minimum, went home...

*[builds to turning point]*

...The difference between where you are and where you want to be isn't talent. It's one more try after you think you're done.

So here's what I'm asking: this week, do the one thing you've been avoiding. Just one. Start there.
