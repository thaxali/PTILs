## Why It Works

Knowing whether a car issue is urgent prevents both dangerous driving and unnecessary panic. Understanding the diagnosis helps you avoid being overcharged at the shop.

## Example Output

**Symptom:** Squealing noise when braking, gets worse downhill.

**Likely cause:** Brake pad wear indicators — the metal tab is touching the rotor.

**Urgency:** Fix within 1-2 weeks. Safe to drive short distances now, but metal-on-metal will damage rotors ($$).

**DIY?** Yes, if you have basic tools. Pads cost $30-50. Shop would charge $150-300.
