## Why It Works

A PRD forces you to think through every dimension of a product before building. Having a structured first draft lets you focus on refining ideas rather than formatting.

## Example Output

**Subject:** TaskFlow — AI-Powered Task Prioritization

**Problem:** Knowledge workers spend 2.5 hours daily deciding what to work on. Current tools list tasks but don't prioritize them.

**User Story:** As a busy PM, I want my task list automatically prioritized by impact and deadline so I can start my day without decision fatigue...
