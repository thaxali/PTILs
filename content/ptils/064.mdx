## Why It Works

Public speaking anxiety often comes from lack of preparation, not lack of talent. Structured coaching on delivery techniques builds confidence through competence.

## Example Output

**Rule of Three:** Structure your talk around 3 key points. Audiences remember 3 things, not 7.

**The Pause:** After a key statement, pause for 2 full seconds. It feels long to you but signals confidence to the audience.

**Open with a question:** 'How many of you have...' gets heads nodding and engagement up immediately.
