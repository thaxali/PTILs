## Why It Works

A structured review framework ensures you consider every angle — performance, value, ecosystem, and alternatives — rather than just first impressions.

## Example Output

**Reviewing: Framework Laptop 16**

**Pros:** Fully modular — swap GPU, ports, even keyboard. Right to repair done right.
**Cons:** Battery life trails MacBook by ~3 hours. Heavier than competitors.
**Verdict:** Best laptop for people who keep hardware 5+ years...
