## Why It Works

Difficult conversations go wrong when we're reactive instead of prepared. Anticipating objections and framing the discussion around shared goals transforms confrontation into collaboration.

## Example Output

**Key points (lead with these):**
1. Start with shared goal: 'We both want this project to succeed'
2. Specific observation: 'The last 3 deadlines were missed by 4+ days'
3. Impact: 'This caused [specific consequence]'

**Likely objections:**
- 'The requirements kept changing' → Acknowledge this is real, then redirect to process
- 'I was waiting on other teams' → Ask what would help unblock faster

**Frame:** This is about the process, not the person.
