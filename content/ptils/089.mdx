## Why It Works

Event planning has dozens of interdependent details that are easy to forget. A comprehensive logistics plan surfaces everything from parking to dietary restrictions before the day arrives.

## Example Output

**Event:** Team offsite, 40 people, 2 days, mountain lodge

**Pre-event:** Dietary survey (send 2 weeks before), transportation signup sheet

**Day 1 Schedule:**
9:00 — Arrival + coffee
9:30 — Icebreaker (not the cringe kind: 'Two truths and a lie' works)
10:00 — Strategy session 1...

**Contingency:** Rain plan for outdoor activities → move to conference room B. Backup catering contact...
