## Why It Works

Giving the AI a specific role as a character provides a clear frame of reference. The AI draws on patterns associated with that expertise, producing more focused, domain-appropriate responses than a generic prompt would.

## Example Output

When given a specific topic or request, the AI responds in character as a character, providing detailed and contextually appropriate responses that demonstrate deep understanding of the role.
