## Why It Works

Interactive fiction exercises creativity for both you and the AI. The constraint of responding only with game narrative creates an immersive, collaborative storytelling experience.

## Example Output

You open your eyes. A bare lightbulb swings above you, casting shadows across a concrete room. There's a heavy metal door to your NORTH and a cracked mirror on the EAST wall. Your pockets are empty. You hear footsteps somewhere above you.
