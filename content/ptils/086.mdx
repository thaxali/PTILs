## Why It Works

Cold emails fail when they're too long, too generic, or ask too much. The constraints force a punchy, personalized message with a clear reason to reply.

## Example Output

**Subject:** Quick question about your auth system

Hi Sarah,

I noticed your team open-sourced your rate limiter last month — really elegant approach to the sliding window problem.

We built something similar for API auth and found a way to cut false positives by 60%. Would a 15-minute call be worth your time to compare approaches?

No pitch, just two engineers geeking out.

— Alex
