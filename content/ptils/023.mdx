## Why It Works

Structured frameworks externalize your reasoning and make trade-offs explicit. The scoring forces you to quantify gut feelings.

## Example Output

**Criteria (weighted):**
1. Cost (25%)
2. Time to implement (20%)...

**Scoring:**
| Option | Cost | Time | ... | Total |
|--------|------|------|-----|-------|
| A      | 8    | 6    | ... | 7.2   |
