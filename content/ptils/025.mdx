## Why It Works

The same information needs different framings for different audiences. This prompt does the translation work for you.

## Example Output

**For executive (30 sec):** We reduced server costs 40% by switching to serverless. No downtime, same performance.

**For teammate:** Instead of paying for servers 24/7, we now only pay when someone actually uses the app...

**For FAQ:** Your app will be just as fast, and we can offer this at a lower price because...
