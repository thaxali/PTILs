## Why It Works

Step-by-step math with explanations of the 'why' — not just the 'how' — builds real understanding rather than rote memorization.

## Example Output

**Problem:** Solve 2x + 5 = 17

**Step 1:** Subtract 5 from both sides → 2x = 12
*Why: We want x alone, so we remove the +5*

**Step 2:** Divide both sides by 2 → x = 6
*Why: 2x means '2 times x', so we undo the multiplication*
