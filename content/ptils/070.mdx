## Why It Works

Dream interpretation through symbolic analysis can reveal subconscious patterns and anxieties. Even if you're skeptical, it's a useful framework for self-reflection.

## Example Output

**Dream:** I was flying over a city but my shoes kept falling off.

**Interpretation:** Flying typically represents freedom or ambition — you may be experiencing a sense of rising above challenges. Losing shoes suggests anxiety about being unprepared or exposed. The combination might reflect excitement about a new opportunity paired with imposter syndrome...
