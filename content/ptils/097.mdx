## Why It Works

Legal language is deliberately precise but practically incomprehensible. Translating to plain English with real-world implications makes the law accessible and actionable.

## Example Output

**Concept: Non-compete agreements**

**Plain English:** Your employer is saying 'After you leave, you can't work for a competitor for X months within Y miles.'

**Practical reality:** These are increasingly unenforceable in many states. California bans them entirely. Even where they're legal, most companies won't spend $50K suing an individual employee...

**What to do:** Don't refuse to sign (it might cost you the job), but know your state's rules.
