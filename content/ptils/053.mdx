## Why It Works

The Socratic method reveals unstated assumptions by following each claim to its logical conclusion. It's uncomfortable but incredibly effective for stress-testing ideas.

## Example Output

**You:** 'I think everyone should learn to code.'

**Socrates:** 'What do you mean by "everyone"? Do you include a 75-year-old retired farmer?'

**You:** 'Well, maybe not everyone, but most young people.'

**Socrates:** 'Why young people specifically? What is it about code that you believe benefits them more than, say, learning a trade?'
