## Why It Works

This overcomes confirmation bias by forcing consideration of opposing views. The balanced assessment at the end synthesizes both perspectives.

## Example Output

**Case in favor:** The data clearly shows...

**Devil's advocate:** However, we should consider...

**Balanced view:** Weighing both sides...
